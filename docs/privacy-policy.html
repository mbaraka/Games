Privacy Policy — Game Hub

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Game Hub ("the app") is a collection of small games published by Baraka. This policy covers what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data collection

Game Hub does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs.

Data stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device only, using Android's standard SharedPreferences storage:

  • Game progress, scores, and settings for each game
  • How many times each game has been launched (used to show a "Most Played" list)
  • Parental-control screen-time limits, if you choose to set any

None of this ever leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

Some games request device permissions needed for their specific feature, for example:

  • NFC — used only by the Key Reader tool to scan NFC tags you choose to scan.
  • Bluetooth — used only by games with two-phone local co-op (e.g. Minesweeper, Train Survivor) to connect directly to another phone running the same app. No data goes through any server.

No permission is used to collect data for any purpose other than the feature it enables.

Children

Game Hub does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the GitHub repository.
